## 1. The company in one paragraph

SLGC Berhad is a Malaysian **investment holding company** whose subsidiaries provide **building construction services** (external and ancillary works) — residential (landed & high-rise), and non-residential (factories, warehouses). Track record since **2009**, expanding from Johor into Melaka, Negeri Sembilan, Selangor and Kuala Lumpur. Its main operating subsidiary, SLG Construction, is a **CIDB G7 contractor** (can bid for projects of any size / unlimited value in Malaysia). It moved into **design-and-build** in 2021. Quality accreditations: ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018.

- Incorporated: 19 April 2024 (as SLGC Sdn Bhd); converted to public company 25 June 2025.
- Managing Director: **Yong Zhen Lin** (23+ years' industry experience), the controlling shareholder.

## 4. Financial performance (audited)

FYE = financial year ended; FPE 2026 = a partial period (interim). All in RM'000.

| **PAT margin** | 0.51% | 2.68% | 6.98% | 4.32% | 4.03% |

**Reading the trend:**
- **Revenue** was flattish ~RM214–230m for three years, then jumped to **RM324m in FYE 2025** (+41%).
- **Profitability improved dramatically** — margins rose from razor-thin (0.5% PAT margin in 2022) to healthier levels (~7% in 2024). Peak net profit was FYE 2024 (RM16.0m).
- Note: net profit dipped slightly from FYE 2024 → FYE 2025 (RM16.0m → RM14.0m) even as revenue surged, i.e. margins compressed on the bigger revenue base.
- Financials were **audited with no qualifications** and no exceptional items.

## 5. Order book & customer concentration

- **Unbilled contract value / order book: ~RM1.00 billion** as at the latest practicable date, to be recognised progressively over the next ~5 financial years — a solid revenue runway.
- **Heavy customer concentration:** top 5 customers contributed **97.23%, 95.69%, 102.58%*, 70.94% and 78.09%** of revenue across the review periods. (*Over 100% reflects revenue reversals/adjustments among other customers.) This concentration is a real risk.

## 8. Competitive strengths (per the company)
1. Established track record in building construction since 2009; CIDB G7 contractor.
2. Quality focus — ISO 9001/45001; uses IBS (industrialised building systems), aluminium formwork; QLASSIC scores 79–80%.
3. Technology adoption — AutoCAD/BIM, Glodon, OpenSpace, Speedbrick.
4. Experienced management team led by 23-year-veteran MD.

**Growth strategies:** (a) buy construction machinery/equipment (aluminium formwork, passenger hoists) within 24 months; (b) upgrade Speedbrick software for better reporting/forecasting.

---

## 9. Key risks (summarised from the prospectus)
- **Cost overruns** on fixed-price/lump-sum contracts squeezing margins.
- **Dependence on winning new projects** to replenish the order book (revenue is project-based, mostly non-recurring, 2–5 year projects).
- **Completion delays** → liquidated damages (LD).
- **Slow collections / receivables & retention-sum recovery** risk (clients on 30–90 day credit).
- **Defects liability claims** during 12–30 month defects liability periods.
- **High customer concentration** (top 5 = ~70–100% of revenue).
- **High gearing** historically.

## 10. Dividends
- **No formal dividend policy.** Intends to pay dividends in future, at the Board's discretion.
- Past dividends: RM2.30m (FYE2022), RM14.76m (FYE2024); none in FYE2023, FYE2025, or FPE2026.

---

## Bottom line (neutral framing)
SLGC is a **profitable, growing Malaysian building contractor** with a **~RM1bn order book** listing on the ACE Market at **RM0.28/share (~11.2x PE, RM156.8m market cap)**. Positives: strong revenue growth into FYE2025, improving margins, large order backlog, debt reduction from IPO proceeds. Watch-items: **very high gearing, thin current ratio, heavy customer concentration, and project-based/non-recurring revenue**. Post-IPO the founder retains ~60% control.
